Thank you for your interest in joining my research group. 

I typically recruit <span class="emp"><b>2–3 outstanding PhD students each year</b></span>. Our research spans both <span class="emp"><b>theoretical and empirical machine learning</b></span>, with a particular focus on data-centric and optimization-guided methods for modern foundation models. If you have a <b>strong background in machine learning, optimization, or related areas</b> (with publications in NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AISTATS, AAAI, UAI, etc) and are excited about our research (please see our lab's [recent publications](https://baharanm.github.io/publications/)), I encourage you to apply to the Computer Science PhD program at UCLA and mention my name in your application.

Due to the large number of inquiries I receive, I am generally unable to meet with prospective applicants before the application deadline. However, you are welcome to email me <b>before or after submitting your application</b> with the subject line "PhD Application". Please include:

- your CV,
- your academic transcript(s), and
- a brief description of your background, research experience, and research interests.

While I may not be able to respond to every email, sending this information helps ensure that I am aware of your application during the review process.
